QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Answers are based on information published by Central Campus of Technology and its programme materials.

What is CCT BUZZ?

CCT BUZZ is a student-focused resource layer built around CCT: faculty pages, learning resources, quizzes and useful campus information. It is intended to complement—not replace—the official campus website.

Where is Central Campus of Technology located?

The official campus site lists Central Campus of Technology at Dharan-14, Hattisar, Sunsari, Nepal.

Which programmes are associated with CCT?

The official CCT site lists departments/programmes including BSc CSIT, BIT, Food Technology, Microbiology, Geology and B.Sc. areas; its brochure also lists B.Tech Food Technology, B.Sc., B.Sc. CSIT, BIT, M.Tech Food Technology and M.Sc. Microbiology.

Is CCT part of Tribhuvan University?

Yes. The official campus site describes CCT as a constituent campus of Tribhuvan University.

Where should I verify official notices?

Always verify admission, examination, result, fee and administrative information against the official CCT website before acting on it.
